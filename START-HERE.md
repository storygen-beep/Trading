# Start here

This is your trading helper. You talk to Claude in plain words, and it does the rest.

## How to begin

Open the Claude app → **Code** → start a session on this repo, then type:

- **Start my day**: find stocks worth watching, check the news, and plan a trade
- **Check my trade**: how your open trade is doing
- **News on TSLA**: any stock you want
- **How am I doing**: today's results

The very first time, Claude asks a few questions about your money and your
limits. If you don't know an answer, say "use the suggestion".

## What to expect

- Claude **never buys or sells without you**. Before every order it shows the
  stock, the cost and **the most you can lose in dollars**, then asks you to
  type **yes**. Then the app shows a button: **Allow once** or **Deny**.
  Only tap Allow once if you really mean it. Never choose "always allow".
- It tells you whether the order is **PRACTICE** or **REAL MONEY** every time.
- Claude can't watch prices while the app is closed. Come back and say
  **check my trade**, and always before **3:55 pm New York time**, because every
  trade gets closed by the end of the day.
- When your daily loss limit is hit, trading stops for the day. That's on purpose.

## Golden rules

1. Only trade money you can afford to lose completely.
2. Never type passwords or broker keys into the chat.
3. Don't change your limits after a bad day. Talk to whoever set this up first.
4. The odds Claude gives are estimates, not promises. Most day traders lose money.
5. If something looks broken, tell whoever set this up what Claude said.
